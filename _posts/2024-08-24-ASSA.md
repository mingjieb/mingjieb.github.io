In 2024, I attended Asian Social Simulation Association Annual Conference and presented my work in BIGAI, "UV-Based Agent Modeling and Learning from Population Data Reconstruction". This work applied the UV theory from our Director Prof. Song-chun Zhu, to model human agent in social simulator, aiming to recover the value-driven individual daily itinerary from the population-level statistic data.
