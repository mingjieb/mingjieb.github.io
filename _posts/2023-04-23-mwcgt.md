In 2023, I attened The Midwest Workshop on Control and Game Theory in Minneapolis, MN, USA, with my collegues Max Wu. It was a great experience to attend several presentations, discussing control theory, game theory, and various applications of distributed systems, such as wind power, social media, etc. 

I also presented poster titled "Distributed Decision-Making in Disrupted Industrial Environments Using a Multi-Agent Framework". It is a summary of the general multi-agent framework I designed to model the scheduling and planning problems in manufacturing systems and supply chain networks.
