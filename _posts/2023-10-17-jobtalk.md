Before I returned to China, I gave a job talk titled "Distributed Decision-Making Using Multi-Agent Framework in Complex Systems" as my final interview for a research scientist position in Multi-agent Lab at Beijing Institute for General Artificial Intelligence. In this talk, I presented my multi-agent framework for manufacturing systems and supply chain networks, as well as a general MARL architecture design to address the chanllenges in the CivRealm project (an environment to play Civilization).
